Estates General
The Estates General of 1789 convened the clergy, nobility, and Third Estate at Versailles.

Tennis Court Oath
The National Assembly swore the Tennis Court Oath not to disband before writing a constitution.

Storming of the Bastille
On 14 July 1789 revolutionaries stormed the Bastille fortress in Paris.

Declaration of Rights
The Declaration of the Rights of Man proclaimed liberty, equality, and popular sovereignty.

Reign of Terror
The Reign of Terror under Robespierre sent thousands to the guillotine in 1793 and 1794.

Thermidorian Reaction
The Thermidorian Reaction arrested Robespierre and ended the Terror in July 1794.

Napoleon rise
Napoleon Bonaparte seized power in the coup of 18 Brumaire in 1799.

Napoleonic Code
The Napoleonic Code standardized civil law across France and its territories.

Women march Versailles
In October 1789 thousands of women marched on Versailles demanding bread and reforms.

Execution of Louis XVI
Louis XVI was executed by guillotine in January 1793 after the monarchy fell.

Directory government
The Directory ruled France from 1795 until Napoleon's coup replaced it.

Congress of Vienna
The Congress of Vienna redrew Europe after Napoleon's defeat in 1815.
