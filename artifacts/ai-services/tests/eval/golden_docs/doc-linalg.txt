Vectors
A vector is an ordered list of numbers describing magnitude and direction.

Dot product
The dot product of two vectors equals the sum of pairwise products and measures alignment.

Matrices
A matrix is a rectangular array of numbers with rows and columns.

Matrix multiplication
Matrix multiplication composes linear maps and is not commutative in general.

Determinant
The determinant measures how a matrix scales volume; zero means singular.

Eigenvalues
Eigenvectors keep their direction under a matrix; eigenvalues give the stretch factor.

Linear independence
Vectors are linearly independent when no one is a combination of the others.

Rank
The rank of a matrix is the dimension of its column space.

Inverse matrix
A square matrix is invertible exactly when its determinant is nonzero.

Orthogonality
Orthogonal vectors have a zero dot product and meet at right angles.

Projections
Projecting a vector onto another uses the dot product divided by the squared norm.

Cross product
The cross product of two 3D vectors is perpendicular to both, scaled by the sine of the angle.
